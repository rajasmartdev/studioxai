🔒 How we handle your information

Who we are

BeyondA1C is a program of the Boston Diabetes Research Institute, a division of MedicaMetrix, Inc. We operate beyonda1c.com and related digital properties. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or interact with us.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide directly: When you fill out our provider inquiry form, send us an email, or request a consultation, we collect your name, organization, email address, phone number, and any other details you choose to share.
  • Usage information: When you visit our website, we automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URL, and device information.
  • Communications: If you email us, we retain those communications to respond to your inquiry and improve our service.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry or consultation request
  • To provide information about BeyondA1C products and services
  • To send updates or educational content you have requested
  • To analyze website usage and improve the site experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our website

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers, MedicaMetrix affiliates, or as required by law.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Inquiry and contact form submissions are typically retained for up to three years.

Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews.

Questions? Contact our privacy team at privacy@beyonda1c.com or write to us at Boston Diabetes Research Institute, 103 Fox Road, Suite 611, Waltham, MA 02451.

HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices  |  Last updated: April 2025

🏥 Notice of Privacy Practices

Important: This notice applies to situations where BeyondA1C or MedicaMetrix receives protected health information (PHI) as a business associate of a covered healthcare entity. The BeyondA1C website itself does not collect health information from individual patients.

Our role under HIPAA

BeyondA1C and MedicaMetrix may function as a Business Associate under HIPAA when we provide services to covered healthcare entities, such as hospitals, clinics, or physician practices.

How we use and disclose PHI

As a business associate, we may use PHI only to perform our contracted services. We do not sell PHI. We implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect PHI from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Your rights

Your rights regarding your health information are primarily managed by your healthcare provider. If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at hhs.gov/ocr.
