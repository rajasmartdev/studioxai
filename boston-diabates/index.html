Symptom Checker

Are You Experiencing Any of These Symptoms?

Burning Sensation in Feet

A persistent heat or burning feeling in the soles, often worse at night.

Tingling or Pins-and-Needles

Electric or prickling sensations along the feet, toes, or lower legs.

Numbness in Feet or Toes

Reduced or absent sensation making it hard to feel pressure or temperature.

Night-time Foot Pain

Aching or stabbing discomfort that intensifies during rest or sleep.

Difficulty Maintaining Balance

Unsteadiness or falls due to reduced proprioception in the feet.

Loss of Sensation While Walking

Inability to feel the ground, increasing risk of unnoticed injuries.

Leg Discomfort or Unusual Sensations

Crawling, pressure, or strange sensations in the legs without clear cause.

If you experience one or more of these symptoms, consider a specialist evaluation. Early nerve assessment can prevent serious foot complications linked to diabetes.

Why It Matters

Why Early Evaluation Matters

Many people assume burning feet or numbness is simply part of aging.

However, diabetes can affect the nerves over time, reducing sensation and increasing the risk of unnoticed injuries and foot complications.

Early assessment helps:

  • Identify nerve damage earlier
  • Understand symptom severity
  • Monitor progression
  • Create a personalized management plan

Advanced Diagnostics

DPN Check — quantitative nerve testing at the clinic.

Diabetic peripheral neuropathy often goes undiagnosed in standard consultations. Subjective tests like monofilament examination can miss early-stage nerve damage when intervention is most effective.

DPN Check is a point-of-care, quantitative nerve conduction study. It produces an objective measure of total nerve function in minutes, helping the consultant identify and stage neuropathy with precision standard tests cannot match.

Quantitative results — not subjective interpretation
Sural nerve amplitude and conduction velocity in minutes
Helps identify neuropathy at clinically significant stages
On-site at Gachibowli — no external referral or wait
