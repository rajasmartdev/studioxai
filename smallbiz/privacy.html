Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Small Biz Enter respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with Australian privacy law.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in our forms or by phone: your name, business name, email, phone number, website, service area, and your Google Ads needs and budget range.
  • Technical and marketing information: your device and browser type, pages viewed, and where you came from (for example the ad or campaign you clicked).

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide a free account review.
  • To provide, manage and improve our services.
  • To understand how our website and advertising perform.
  • To contact you about your enquiry. We will only send marketing messages if you ask for them or agree to them, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with trusted providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, customer management and analytics or advertising tools. We may also share it if the law requires.

Cookies and advertising

This website uses cookies and similar tools, including Google Ads and Google Analytics, to measure visits and the results of our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Google account.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and to keep it only as long as we need it. No online system is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information through our forms.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Please contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, please get in touch.
