Our Franchise Model

What is the total store setup cost?

The total store setup cost including franchise fees is estimated at INR 10,00,000. This covers the franchise fee (INR 5,00,000), interior & exterior cost for 150 sq. ft. (INR 2,50,000), and equipment cost (INR 2,50,000).

What is the required store area?

The store area required is between 150 to 300 sq. ft. We offer both kiosk and standalone models with brand-promoting lively interiors, designed to attract customers and create a memorable Yummpy's experience.

What franchise models does Yummpy's offer?

Yummpy's offers both unit franchise and master franchise models. Both are strategically planned and promising to invest in, with full setup, training, and ongoing operational support provided by our team.

Where is Yummpy's currently present?

Yummpy's currently has outlets in Hyderabad, Warangal, and Amalapuram. We are actively expanding across India and are looking for passionate franchise partners in new cities and towns.
