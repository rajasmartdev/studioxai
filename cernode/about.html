About

The electrode line from Alera Medtech.

Cernode covers the everyday contact points of clinical neurodiagnostics, built on one specification system and a shared color code that keeps selection quick and reordering simple for the stockroom.

Our approach

A complete neurodiagnostic electrode line, engineered in house and made to one standard.

From concentric and monopolar EMG needles to a full hydrogel surface family, Cernode runs on a single specification language. Proprietary coatings, adhesives and needle geometry are developed to make testing more comfortable for patients and safer for the clinicians who run it.
