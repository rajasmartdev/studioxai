About the line

Cernode is the electrode line from Alera Medtech, built around the everyday contact points of clinical neurodiagnostics.

Concentric and monopolar needles for electromyography, and a hydrogel surface family for EEG, evoked potentials and nerve conduction studies. The lines run on one specification system and a shared color code, which keeps selection quick and reordering simple for the stockroom.

Concentric EMG Needles

Single-use concentric needle electrodes for electromyography. The recording electrode sits inside the cannula and is referenced to it, so each needle carries a small, defined recording area. A stainless steel core and a tungsten core are offered across five lengths, color-coded by gauge and length.

Hydrogel Surface Electrodes

Ag/AgCl surface electrodes on a qualified hydrogel, for EEG, evoked potentials, nerve conduction studies and surface EMG. The family runs from cup electrodes and nerve conduction pad sets through large recording pads, ground and stimulation pads, and extended-wear and pediatric options. All are supplied non-sterile for single-patient use, with DIN 1.5 mm or 3.5 mm snap connectors.

Changing the paradigm for neurodiagnostic electrodes.

Cernode pairs one specification system with proprietary electrode technology, built to make neurodiagnostic testing more comfortable for patients and safer for the clinicians who run it.

Proprietary technology

Next generation adhesives, hydrophilic coatings, high-insulation base coatings, and the shared coding are developed in house across the needle and surface lines.

Patient comfort

Next generation adhesives to alleviate patient trauma during removal; a hydrophilic lubricious coating and dual-facet needle geometry to limit insertion and removal trauma through the course of a study.

Clinician safety

Detachable hubs help prevent accidental needle-stick injuries.
