Our Story

Where It All Began…

Remember those late nights in the hostel, surviving on one thing that never let you down? Maggi. It's everyone's comfort food — a taste of home no matter where you are.

"Yummpy's was born when a few passionate foodies decided to create novel, lip-smacking dishes using Maggi — adding a twist of flavour, and a whole lot of love."

Ever since our launch in 2016, we've worked with experienced chefs to create new and fresh flavours. We strive to provide high-quality, hygienic food that is quick to serve and easy on the pocket — much to the delight of our customers.
