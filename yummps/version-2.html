01 — Our Story

Where comfort food becomes
an experience.

Remember that late night in the hostel, surviving on one thing that never let you down? Maggi. It's everyone's comfort food — a taste of home no matter where you are.

"We decided to create novel, lip-smacking dishes using Maggi — adding a twist of flavour, and a whole lot of love."

We've worked with experienced chefs to craft new and fresh flavours since 2016. Every bowl is made with high-quality ingredients, hygienically prepared, and served fast — because you shouldn't have to wait long for something this good.
